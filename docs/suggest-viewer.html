Suggest viewer

Real London streets around the Byron demo spot, running the real suggestRoutes from @knockd/shared. Rebuild with pnpm rebuild:suggest-viewer.

Map tool
Brush radius: 60 m
Crew, seconds per door, seconds per road crossing people s/door s/crossing
Drag on the map to select houses.
Dashed line = a group's route; its dashes cycle through the members' colours (a pair alternates two). Routes that share a street sit side by side. Untick a group to hide its route, or press "only". Black dot = car. Red = regroup pin. White ring = meeting point; a dotted line is a group's walk there after their last door. The car and the pin snap onto the nearest street. Small coloured dot = where a group finishes. Selected houses take the colour of the person who knocks them (blue = selected, not planned yet). Solos do one side of a street, then the other (crossing costs ~15 s); pairs take one side each and leapfrog where one side has more doors. The number on a house is its place in that rep's own order (zoom in to see them). A coloured U-turn badge marks every spot where a route turns back on itself.
